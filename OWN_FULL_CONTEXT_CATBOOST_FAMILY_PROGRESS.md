# Complete tree-process family resource accounting

The family accounting component sums the root worker and all scientific
descendants selected by the unchanged original native research-peer observer.
It reserves the complete16GiB family budget independently in physical-footprint
and RSS domains, while retaining the original24GiB aggregate limits,18.75GiB
disk floor, full output reserve, compressor thresholds and128MiB swap-growth
limit against the original baseline. Three stable samples and same-process
continuation remain requirements of the future owned controller.

Forty-one combined tree-component checks pass. New fixtures reject summed
family overcaps even when each individual role is smaller, independent-domain
reservation mistakes, peer/ancestry/start-identity races and roles outside the
dedicated group. An orphan-stop fixture verifies that a surviving captured
role permits stopping the original whole group after root loss, while a
recycled PID never authorizes a signal. These signalling checks use mocks;
no learned production process was stopped by the tests.

A separate actual native probe launched a small parent and two helper children
in one group. The original observer measured all three; combined physical/RSS
usage was20,483,200/34,684,928bytes. A full16GiB reservation fit the original
limits at that observation. The helpers exited normally after the probe.
This does not establish production peak memory or authorize a future fit.

The component does not create a queue, change observer scope, relax resource
limits, launch model training or release either original lane. Still required
are the owned producer/consumer controller and role/exit/resource gates, both
full studies and independent raw/model/canonical replays, selected qualifying
bag and own18 calibration/fixed audits. The existing RBM400 data/neural/bag/
own17 chain keeps priority.
