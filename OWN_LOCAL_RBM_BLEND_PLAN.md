# Complete local RBM addition to our own blend

This separately named study adds our full locally trained bipolar RBM400 as a
sixteenth family to every original own15 family. It does not depend on a remote
weekend return, and does not change any live producer or existing frozen plan.
There is no production result or new score yet.

The owned queue waits for full original own15 canonical completion and the local
RBM's two15-epoch models, actual fresh-MPS full-width qualification, original63
payload/row/projection/zero-target admission and complete saved-model/native
query bit replays. Every original old family, old model and new model remains
bound to its independently verified full source closure. Downloaded prediction
features and recovered outcomes are excluded from the fitting closure. Original
public source code and the previously verified synthetic zero-target CSVs are
permitted; no expected data hashes are rebased.

The primary is declared now: `local-rbm16-simplex`. All16 coefficients are fitted
on the original1,128,164 known labels using uniform-initialized SLSQP with
nonnegative weights summing to one, ftol1e-17 and at most1000 iterations. A signed
ridge control uses the released lambda10 and an unpenalized intercept; its full

simplex predictions without refitting. All six known/qualifying vectors are
frozen before any new recovered audit. The primary is never selected by those
audit outcomes. Apparent known calibration error is not nested cross-validation.

An independent verifier uses compensated products, enumerates all65,535
nonempty simplex supports, verifies the actual coefficients' objective and KKT
conditions even when coefficients are nonunique, independently solves every
ridge equation, and checks every f32 bit of all six vectors. The own15 control
must retain the original hashes. Complete NPY vectors from the RBM and raw f32
vectors from the old families are read using authenticated file descriptors,
with exact schema, identity, population and finite-value checks.

Only after full preaudit does a separate process open the immutable recovered
label blob47ed4788ffba11aad029854a8e60d4dee04fc899. All three fixed candidates
are audited on the full1,408,789/1,408,342 groups. A further independent process
reconstructs the frozen arithmetic and computes canonical compensated scores.
The target remains strictly below0.85725 on both groups. These are adaptive
community audits, not official or untouched Test scores.

All scientific phases run serially on the original primary lane with two CPU
threads and a2GiB worker physical/RSS cap. Research totals remain below24GiB in
both metrics, compressor below5GiB (admission below4.5GiB), swap growth at most
128MiB from one original scientific baseline, and disk above18.75GiB plus all
remaining outputs and64MiB reserve. Three stable samples precede dispatch and
same-process continuation. Resource measurement failures fail closed. Resource
stops preserve the same process, stack, owner/watchdog, lane descriptor and
baseline; no retry, refit, family truncation, cap increase or baseline reset.
The watchdog retains its inherited lane descriptor if the owner disappears,
including when writing recovery evidence fails, until the actual child is
terminal. Outputs are exclusive, complete, fsynced and never overwritten.

Entry point: `scripts/upstream_local_rbm_blend_queue.py --start` after reviewed
protocol checks and source freeze. Importing modules does not start work.
